        About CYBERTECH Architecture

        CYBERTECH is an advanced prototype developed for Analysing email threats. It addresses the critical need for deep forensic tracing of fraudulent emails. By correlating email headers, IP reputation, geolocation, and AI-driven NLP sentiment analysis, the platform provides unprecedented visibility into malicious infrastructure. The platform reconstructs relay paths, uncovers spoofed domains, and generates cryptographically sealed forensic evidence to support incident response and legal workflows. Designed to combat sophisticated phishing, BEC, and impersonation attacks, CYBERTECH empowers security teams with actionable intelligence.

          How is the Threat Score Calculated?

          Overview of the CyberTech Heuristic Engine

          The overall Threat Percentage is generated dynamically using a multi-layered heuristic weighting system. When an email is scanned, it passes through 4 core analysis layers:

          1. Authentication Penalties (RFC Check - 30% Weight) If the sending domain fails Sender Policy Framework (SPF) or DomainKeys Identified Mail (DKIM) checks, the base threat score immediately increases by 20% to 30% per failure, as this indicates a high likelihood of Domain Spoofing.
          2. Natural Language Processing (NLP Cues - 35% Weight) The text body and subject line are parsed for Social Engineering keywords (e.g., "Urgent", "Suspend", "Password", "Wire Transfer"). Each matched keyword adds 10% to the score due to phishing behavior patterns.
          3. Lexical Link & Domain Typosquatting (20% Weight) Embedded hyperlinks are analyzed using Levenshtein distance against banking and major brand targets. High-risk TLDs like `.su`, `.cc`, `.ru` contribute to malicious weighting.
          4. Infrastructure, VPN & IP Reputation (15% Weight) If the originating IP lacks standard routing headers, or is traced to known high-risk offshore VPNs/Datacenters, a severity multiplier is applied. Legitimate traffic from known IPs (like Google or Amazon) naturally lowers the score.
